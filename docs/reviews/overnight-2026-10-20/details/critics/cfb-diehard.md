# TappedIn feedback: die-hard CFB fan, full Saturday slate (cfb-diehard)

How I tested: a fresh worktree on origin/main (3dce3ca). I looked at onboarding, then skipped it with the setup.json trick. I watched the whole day rebuilt from finals (`--replay 20261017 --synth --from 11:55 --speed 10`, noon to midnight), then the real recording for the night games (`--replay 20261017 --from 19:30 --speed 20`). I tried Low, then High, then Low again, sound "Follows the action", locks (button and **L**), **H**, fullscreen with **3**/Esc, the big and mini tickers, halftime reels, the game-over picker and auto-pick. I read every decision in the log, and also ran `npm run sim -- --day 20261017 --synth --from 12:00 --compare`. Times below are replay ET.

## What's good
- Break detection at Low is mostly right. End-of-quarter, injury and kickoff-after-score fills are 2 to 4 minutes long. I never saw it switch on an incompletion or a penalty. Every decision has a readable reason ("Timeout: ~1:46 left (80%), quiet 77s"), which a nerd like me loves.
- The halftime reel with real ESPN clips (score bug, "highlight 1 of 21") is excellent. Coming back for the 2nd half kickoff worked on the noon games. (04)
- The big ticker on the real recording is the best part for a CFB fan: ranks (#2 ND, #8 BYU, #18 OKST), logos, possession, TOUCHDOWN/RED ZONE tags, 2OT. (08)
- Per-screen fullscreen is instant. Sound follows it in and goes back on exit. Lock, **L**, **H**, **1-4**, Esc and **T** all do what the README says.
- Fill-in picks prefer red-zone games and say why. The game-over picker lists the same sport first, with the score, the clock and which screen already has each game. (06)

## What's confusing
- **"Xfinity sign-in failed" red chip in REPLAY mode** (medium). Replay and dry run show data tiles, yet a red failure chip sits in the top bar (and in the big ticker's label) all day. It reads as if the app is broken. In replay/dry run it should say nothing, or "no video in replay". (all screenshots)
- **The log says "you picked X as home" for the startup auto-pick** (low). At 11:55:02 all four screens log `USER_SET_HOME … you picked IPS @ MNC as home`, but I picked nothing. That makes the decisions log misleading when you check why a screen has a game.
- **Picking a game on a locked screen silently unlocks it** (medium). At 15:44:29 S3 went `LOCKED FLA @ TEX -> HOME AUB @ UGA`. Twenty seconds later it was auto-switched away (see bugs). I locked that screen to keep it mine; changing the game shouldn't drop the lock without saying so.
- **`--from 12:00` on the real 10/17 recording shows four black screens and "0 live" with no explanation** (medium). That recording starts at 18:31 ET. Nothing in the UI or log says "recording starts 6:31 PM; use --synth for the day games". I only found out by unzipping the file.
- **Big ticker label is truncated** (low): "SAT OCT 17 · MY SPO…" and "REPLAY SIGN-IN FAILE". (05, 08)
- **The startup pick put soccer on screen 1 on a CFB Saturday** (medium). At 11:55 screen 1 got Ipswich vs Man City at 90'+8' (two minutes from full time), next to three noon CFB kickoffs. The sim's default homes do the same. On a fall Saturday the default should be four CFB games. (02)

## Slow or clunky
- **Sound "Follows the action" is far too jumpy** (high). It switched 64 times in about 3.5 game-hours (12:20 to 15:53), sometimes every 30-60 s (13:30:01, 13:31:00, 13:31:49, 13:32:48, 13:33:22, 13:34:26, 13:35:25…). It often jumped to a red-zone trip in a blowout ("MSST @ LSU: red zone, margin 21" at 13:21, 13:27, 13:31). And it ignores my locked team: Texas tied in the red zone in the 4th quarter, and the sound still left for other games. It needs a minimum hold time (2+ minutes), no audio for blowouts, and "my locked screen wins ties".
- **High sensitivity is unwatchable** (medium). In 30 game-minutes (15:48-16:20) screens changed constantly. S1: 16:04:06 GT@VT → 16:04:26 PSU@MICH → 16:05:13 GT@VT. S2: 16:18:55 ALA@TENN → 16:19:16 MIZ@MISS → 16:19:43 GT@VT → 16:20:24 home: four channel changes in 90 s, which is about 18 s of "Switching…" card with real video. The sim agrees: High is 32.6 switches per screen-hour and 170 min of home action missed, against Low's 16.1 and 83. The Settings description "jumps to big moments" undersells what it does.
- **End-of-quarter pile-ups** (medium). All the noon games reach the end of a quarter at the same moment, and every screen runs through the leftovers. At 14:41-14:43, S1 went PITT → FLA@TEX (dup) → KU@KSU → FAU@ARMY in 72 s, and S4 went GAST@JMU → FLA@TEX → CHSO@CLEM in 21 s. When every game breaks at once, "stay home" is the better pick.
- **Pressing H only buys about 20 s** (low). At 16:24 I pressed **H** on S2 and it went home. Two seconds later the log said "would switch to ALA @ TENN; 8s dwell left". "Back to my game" should mean "for this break".

## Doesn't work as intended (bugs)
- **Screens fill in with a game that's already on another screen, and end up as 3-4 copies of one game** (high). On the real recording at 19:32:13, S1, S2 and S3 all switched to `TCU @ BAY [DUPLICATE: only option]`, which is S4's home, so all four screens showed TCU @ BAY. At 16:24 (synth), S2 and S4 both showed AUB @ UGA, S3's home: three screens of one game. Worse, auto-pick makes that duplicate the screen's new *home*. By 23:24 three screens had home NAVY @ UTSA, and at 23:45 all four had home BOS @ LA (NHL). That got saved, so `.state/settings.json` had `screenSports: nhl,nhl,nhl,nhl` for my next start. Repro: synth day, leave it running unattended past 23:00, then `grep AUTO_PICK`. A copy of a game I can already see is worth nothing; stay home instead (`allowDuplicates` is on by default).
- **Game-over auto-pick took a 20-point blowout over a tied game** (high). At 15:23 the S4 picker showed CIN @ WVU **42-42, 4th 3:06** first, under the headline "Best live game in 1:08". At 15:25:03 it auto-picked `GAST @ JMU (margin 20, 2:58 - 4th)`, with FAU @ ARMY (margin 32) as the runner-up. CIN @ WVU wasn't considered, probably because it was in a break at that instant. A break in a tied game's last 3 minutes is short. Auto-pick should choose the game I'll want for the next half hour, not whatever happens to be in play at that second. (06)
- **The screen stays on the fill-in after my game resumes ("home is back; 17-20s left on the substitute first")** (high). In the final minutes of close games, that means missing my own game's snaps. It happened 19 times on the night recording alone. 15:26:14: PITT @ BC, a one-score game with 1:33 left, resumed and S1 stayed 17 s on CIN @ WVU. 15:28:25: same again, 17 s. 13:35:37: MSST @ LSU resumed and S4 stayed 18 s. 20:31:51: TCU @ BAY at 0:58 of the 2nd, 20 s. `minSubstituteSeconds` should never outrank "home has a new play", at least in the 4th quarter or the last 2 minutes of a half.
- **It switches while the break forecast says the break is nearly over** (medium). At 15:44:51 S3 went `BREAK_FILL … BREAK? 0.11 (11% chance of 60s+) ~0:25 left`, 22 s after I picked that game, and came back 46 s later. Other examples: 16:14:10 `BREAK? 0.49 (49% chance of 45s+)` at High, and 19:32:13 `BREAK? 0.30`. The README rule is "switch when P ≥ confidence", but once a game is in the BREAK state it seems to fill no matter the current probability.
- **The fill-in went to halftime and the screen stayed on it for 2.5 minutes** (medium). At 13:44:12 S2 filled with KU @ KSU, which went to HALFTIME at 13:44:41. S2 sat on a halftime show until 13:47:13 ("home OSU @ IU has only ~93s of break left; staying"), even though OSU was at the IU 2 near the end of the half. (05)
- **Halftime flip-flop** (medium, real recording). S4 TCU @ BAY: 20:38:08 HALFTIME (reel) → 20:39:26 `HALFTIME_OVER 2nd half under way: TCU 7-6 BAY Halftime` → 20:39:46 HALFTIME again. That's reel → game → reel in 100 s, while the data still said Halftime.
- **A soccer full time was treated as halftime** (low for me). At 12:03:17 IPS @ MNC at 90'+12' went `LIVE -> HALFTIME`, and S1 played a halftime reel for a finished match instead of the game-over picker.
- **Replay and dry run wait for a real Xfinity sign-in before showing anything** (high for anyone trying it without an account). On a fresh profile, `--replay` opens the real Xfinity On Campus page and the window sits at "Choose a game / 0 live" for up to **10 minutes** (`waitForUserMs: 600000`) until that times out. `electron/main.cjs` runs `handoff()` before `createViews()` unless `--signin=defer`, and replay doesn't pass defer. Closing the sign-in window didn't help. I worked around it with `.state/providers/xfinity-on-campus.json` `{"signin":{"waitForUserMs":3000,…}}`. The first-run onboarding in replay mode also opens the real sign-in window. (01)
- **Sound "Follows the action" is forgotten on restart** (medium). It went back to "You pick" on the next start. `.state/settings.json` has sensitivity and tickers but no audio mode.
- **Stale tags on the big ticker at halftime** (low). At HALF it still showed "TOUCHDOWN FLA", "RED ZONE" (CIN @ WVU) and "FIELD GOAL K…". (05)
- **Text overlaps on the data tiles** (low, dry run only): the "Live data (dry run) · Locked" pill sits on the last-play line, and the "Halftime highlights" label on the clip caption. The overlay header also lands on the tile's own header while the overlay fades (S2 at 13:39). (03, 04, 07)

## What's missing (for a hardcore CFB fan)
- **The ranker doesn't know close-and-late beats red zone in a blowout** (high). `footballCompare` sorts by "red zone, then margin". A red-zone trip in a 28-point game ranks above a tied game with 2:00 left in the 4th. It happened all day: 14:13:49 `FAU @ ARMY red zone, margin 28` was picked over UVA @ SMU (margin 3), and 15:35:25 `GAST @ JMU red zone, margin 18` over CIN @ WVU (margin 7). The NFL module has "two-minute drill in a one-score game"; CFB needs the same, plus OT. CFB overtime starts at the 25, so an OT possession isn't "red zone" until it crosses the 20.
- **No rankings or upsets in the switching or the game list** (high). The big ticker shows ranks, but the game list, screen headers, the "why" line and the ranker ignore them. A ranked team losing late to an unranked one ("upset alert") is the #1 reason to switch on a Saturday, and it isn't a signal at all. #2 ND vs #8 BYU, tied late, got no more weight than Navy vs UTSA.
- **No "only my sports for fill-ins" switch** (medium). All 14 sports are on by default. At 12:50:18 an end-of-Q1 break sent S4 to `AVL @ NEW — Other sport (EPL): margin 0, 20'` for 4 minutes. At 13:38:48, OSU @ IU (a top-10 game, 2:00 before half) filled with `NJ @ WSH — Other sport (NHL)`. The mini ticker kept rotating to La Liga, MLS and EPL ("MLS · CLT 0-1 PHI · 44'" at 15:23, while CFB had a 42-42 4th quarter). Turning sports off in Settings works, but I'd want "CFB Saturday: CFB only" as one choice, including for the ticker.
- **A locked screen does nothing when its game ends** (low). FLA @ TEX went final 27-24 at 15:24, and S3 stayed on the final tile with no picker. A "your game is over, pick the next one" prompt (no auto-pick) would fit a locked screen.
- **Halftime goes to a reel even when great live games are on** (medium). At 20:38 three screens played reels at once (S1, S2, S4) while ND @ BYU, LOU @ SYR and OKST @ HOU were live. Reels are nice, but during the night window I'd rather see live football at halftime, with the reel as the fallback. (07)
- **No "my team" concept** (medium). Lock is the only tool. I want "Texas": always on screen 3 when it's on TV, its sound wins ties, and the app never covers my team's live snaps with another game's blowout.

## New feature ideas
- **A real "RedZone" screen** (high value). One screen with no home game that always chases the best live moment (red zone, one-score 4th, OT, upset alert), like the TV channel. The other three keep their homes. Right now only the High level's "FILL_UPGRADE" comes close, and only during a home break.
- **Upset alert**: ranked team trailing or tied in the 2nd half against an unranked or lower-ranked team. Use it as a hot moment for the ranker, the sound and a mini-ticker jump.
- **"Close & late" first on Saturdays**: one-score game in the last 5:00 of the 4th, or OT, beats any red-zone trip with a margin above 16.
- **Never duplicate unless I ask**: a duplicate fill or auto-pick should be off by default. If every screen's game is in a break, just stay home.
- **Sound hold time and a "my screen" priority** in Settings → Sound.
- **A "what you missed" chip** when a screen comes back home: "Missed: 1 play, 4-yd run" or "TD while you were away", so a late return is less painful.
- **For replays: show the recording's time range and a speed control in the UI.** It would make checking the switching (what I did all night) much easier.
