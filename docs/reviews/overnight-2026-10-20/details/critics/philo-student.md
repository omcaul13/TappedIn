# TappedIn feedback: "Philo Edu student" (philo-student)

My school gives me Philo Edu, not Xfinity On Campus. I tried to set TappedIn up with that, then fell back to services I could plausibly get: Sling TV (Orange, no locals), YouTube TV, and so on. Sandbox: worktree `critic-philo-student` (origin/main 3dce3ca), LOCALAPPDATA redirected, ports 7504/9404/9454. I never signed in to anything. Onboarding ran in `--setup-demo` (YouTube TV, then Sling). Replays ran with `provider: "sling"` and a hand-made Sling-Orange-style lineup in `.state/lineups/sling.json`: ESPN, ESPN2, SEC Network, ACC Network, TNT, TBS, truTV, NBA TV, NHL Network, MLB Network; no ABC/CBS/NBC/FOX/FS1/ESPNU/BTN/CBSSN. Days replayed: `--replay 20261017` from 19:30 and 20:35, and `--replay 20261019-live`.

## What's good (short)
- The service list is clean. Every service is labelled **Tested** or **Experimental**, and campus TV is grouped apart from streaming services. A school search for USC or BU gives a real one-line answer ("Uses Apogee Stream2…", "BU ended campus TV…").
- Games I can't watch are greyed out with a specific reason: "not in lineup: FS1", "streaming only: ESPN+", "streaming only: ACCNX", "not in lineup: NBC Sports Philadelphia". The sport grid counts only watchable games. (07, 15)
- The game-over picker and fill-ins never offered an unwatchable game. The halftime reel worked on a thin lineup. (11, 12)
- The streams step says it plainly: "Your plan played 3 streams at once (YouTube TV documents 3)… the next one stopped because YouTube TV reported a stream limit." (05)
- Diagnostics is laid out well: five steps, pass or fail, what each step saw, and where fixes are stored. (09)

## What's confusing
- **Philo isn't in the app at all, and searching for it gives a dead end. (high)** Typing "Philo" (or Hulu, Fubo, DirecTV, or "University of Richmond", a Philo Edu school) shows "No service matches. TappedIn supports the services listed when the search is empty; more can be added (see CONTRIBUTING.md)." The README knows why ("Philo, which carries no sports networks"; Hulu, Fubo and DIRECTV are "not supported yet"), but the app never says it. A Philo user should get one line: Philo has no ESPN or sports networks, so TappedIn can't use it; these services work instead. The app user also has no idea where CONTRIBUTING.md is, and it isn't a link. (02)
- **BU's note says "Use a streaming service you subscribe to", and then the list is empty. (medium)** The school hit sits above "No service matches." It should show the streaming-services group right there.
- **"Experimental" is only explained in a hover tooltip. (medium)** Nothing on the page says what it means for me ("nobody has tested this with a real account; Diagnostics can fix it"), and the step-by-step setup never warns me before sign-in. Each service's `notes` in `config/providers/*.json` are never shown anywhere in the UI. YouTube TV's says Google may block sign-in in embedded browsers and that away from home you get that area's local channels. That is exactly what I'd want to read before picking it. (02)
- **The Channels step result flashes by. (high)** The setup runner goes straight from the scan to the playback test (`runner.ts`: `if (this.v.scan) await this.playTest()`). Sampling every second, I never saw the ✓/✗ network grid. It went from "Reading your channel guide…" to "Playback test", and that step only lists the first 6 networks found, with no ✗ list. For a non-Xfinity user, "you don't get FOX, ABC, FS1, Big Ten Network" is the most important thing setup can say, and it isn't readable.
- **The sign-in wording is campus-only on consumer services. (low)** For YouTube TV and Sling it says "It's YouTube TV's own page (school sign-in pages included)."
- **Channel numbers on URL services. (low)** Sling and YouTube TV have no channel numbers, but every header and list shows "ESPN 1", "ESPN2 2", "SEC Network 5", which are just guide-row indexes. (07, 11)
- **The ticker and the game list disagree on the network. (low)** For ANA @ NYR, the big ticker says "ESPN+" and the game list says "not in lineup: MSG". It's the same game with two different reasons.
- **"No games today." when the recording just hasn't started. (low)** `--replay 20261019-live --from 19:30` shows "No games today." with four black screens titled RZ-SCREEN-1..4 until 19:59, when the recording starts. It should say "waiting for data" or start at the first record. The `-live` folder name did work as a `--replay` value. (13)

## Slow or clunky
- **The channel list in Settings → TV service is about 60 networks long, mostly "Not in my lineup". (medium)** That includes every RSN (YES, SNY, MASN, Altitude…), in a fixed catalog order. The 10 I have are scattered through it. It needs "show only mine" or "carried first", and some way to map a guide channel the catalog doesn't know (my "Longhorn Network" row had nowhere to go). (08)
- **The game list and ticker are mostly games I can't watch. (medium)** On 10/17 at 7:35, 10 of 12 live CFB games in screen 1's list were greyed out. On Monday, every NHL and tennis game was. The big ticker doesn't mark unwatchable games at all: WSU @ ORST on USA Net looks the same as ARK @ VAN on ESPN2. A "hide what I can't watch" toggle, or dimming in the ticker, would help a lot. (07, 10)
- **There's no way back from the last setup step. (low)** Once I'm at "How many screens?", the only button is "Start watching". There's no Back or "Choose another service", and the step tracker is hidden on that step. (05)
- **Export says "Send it to the project owner" without saying how. (medium)** It gives no email, no link, and no issue template. Not knowing where to send it is the main reason someone wouldn't send the fix back. (09)

## Doesn't work as intended (bugs)
- **Sign-in detection says "Signed in to Sling" with a brand-new, never-signed-in profile. (high)** Steps: give the app a fresh LOCALAPPDATA, set `provider: "sling"` and a completed `.state/setup.json`, then run `--replay 20261017`. The log shows `Sling sign-in: ok (Signed in to Sling)`. Settings → TV service → Diagnostics → Sign-in detection → Run: ✓ "Signed in: the check page is Sling TV and doesn't look signed out" (url watch.sling.com/dashboard/home, signedOut false). The check only looks for "sign in|log in|get started" in the first 4000 characters of body text. Sling's dashboard, probably the anonymous Freestream page or a page that hasn't rendered yet, passes. Diagnostics' first step shows a false ✓, and that sends people off fixing the wrong step. (09)
- **On a thin lineup, auto-switching just copies other screens. (high)** 10/17 from 20:35 at 10x with 4 screens: **all 57 fill-ins were `[DUPLICATE: only option]`**. At one point three or four screens showed UK @ OU at once (S2 and S4 filling with it while S3 was its home). With 4 carried CFB networks and 4 screens, there's never a non-duplicate. In a grid where I can already see that game, a duplicate fill-in is pointless. With a small lineup, the default should be to stay home, or the app should suggest fewer screens. (11, 12)
- **After a game ends, the auto-pick makes another screen's game this screen's new home. (medium-high)** At 22:27:34, `[S1] AUTO_PICK … -> HOME UK @ OU [DUPLICATE: only option]`. UK @ OU was already S3's home, and WAKE @ CAL was kicking off at 22:30 on ESPN2, the channel S1's finished game had just freed. Then both screens hit game over together at 22:40 and showed two identical pickers. S3 then auto-picked LOU @ SYR, which was S2's home. Two screens ended up with permanent duplicate homes. (12)
- **The picker offers two games on the same channel. (medium)** At 22:41 the game-over picker listed FSU @ MIA "ESPN 1 · S4's game" (in OT) and UTAH @ COLO "ESPN 1" next to each other. Tuning ESPN can only show one of them. The auto-pick seems to avoid it, but a hand pick wouldn't know. (12)
- **Closing Diagnostics restarts the replay from the start. (low)** After running one Diagnostics step and pressing Close, the replay clock jumped back from 19:41 to 19:30 (`replaying 20261017 from 19:30:00` logged again). I didn't check whether live mode has a similar re-init.
- **Empty screens are black, with internal view names. (medium)** On Monday (one watchable game at startup: MNF on ESPN), screens 2–4 stayed black with "RZ-SCREEN-2/3/4" in grey. You only see "Choose a game" if you hover. CLE @ TB on TBS, which I do carry, went live at about 8:08, and no empty screen picked it up for the whole replay. (13, 15)
- **Monday replay: MNF never switched. (low, may be the recording)** In `20261019-live`, WSH @ SF flapped PRE ↔ UNKNOWN ("no ESPN data for 60s") and was UNKNOWN from about 20:15 to 22:17, so there were zero switch decisions all night. That may be gaps in the recording rather than the app, but the user just sees a stuck screen.
- **Halftime reel: the "Halftime highlights" tag overlaps the clip caption at the bottom left. (low)** (11)
- **README has mangled paths from escape characters. (low)** Line 96 has a literal tab in "`config<TAB>appedin.config.json5`". Line 100 has a backspace in "`scripts<BS>uild-installer.ps1`".

## What's missing
- **A "your lineup is thin" moment. (high)** After the scan, tell me: "You get 4 of the 12 national sports networks. On a typical Saturday that's about N watchable games at once, so 2 screens may suit you better." Also offer to follow more sports so fill-ins have somewhere to go (tier 2/3).
- **A list of the games I'm missing, with the reason. (medium)** For example, "tonight 9 games are on networks you don't get: FOX ×2, FS1 ×2, ABC, NBC…", which helps me decide whether to add a service or a network.
- **ESPN+ and other add-ons. (medium)** About a third of the greyed games were "streaming only: ESPN+", "ACCNX" or "MW+". Many students have ESPN+ through Disney+/Hulu bundles. Even "not supported, but you could watch it in the ESPN app" beats a dead row.
- **The `provider` key isn't documented in `config/tappedin.config.json5`. (low)** I had to read `src/config.ts` to learn the key existed.

## New feature ideas
- **Combine two services. (medium)** For example, Sling for ESPN/ACCN/SECN plus an antenna or another service for the locals: per-screen provider, or a "second service" for missing networks.
- **Show what each service carries before sign-in. (medium)** A one-glance "this service usually carries: ESPN, ESPN2, ACCN, SECN; not ABC/CBS/FOX locals on Orange" at the service-pick step would save a sign-in for nothing.
- **Make "Use for all screens" shrink the grid. (low)** If fewer watchable games are live than screens, offer to go to that many screens instead of leaving black ones.
