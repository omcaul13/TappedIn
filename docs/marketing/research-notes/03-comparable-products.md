# 3. How comparable products market themselves

Research agent 3 of 8. Compiled 2026-10-21. Labels: [DATA] = measured or company-reported number; [CONSENSUS] = several independent sources agree; [ANECDOTE] = single case or opinion. Company-reported numbers are marked as such. Reddit could not be fetched directly (crawler blocked), so the demand language below comes from forums, review sites and press reports of social posts.

## 1. Top findings

1. **"Commercial-free" is the most valuable promise in this category, and RedZone just gave it up.** For 16 seasons RedZone opened with "seven hours of commercial-free football." In Sept 2025 Scott Hanson dropped "commercial-free" from that line after split-screen ads tested in Dec 2024 drew backlash and an on-air apology. [DATA] [1][3] That leaves an opening for TappedIn's message ("Every break, a better game"), but TappedIn must never say or suggest "ad-free," since the viewer still sees the service's ads whenever a screen sits on a break.
2. **A college football RedZone does not exist and is a known gap.** ESPN's president said in Sept 2025 that ESPN is "considering" one. A conference commissioner called it "a pretty difficult hill to climb" because rights are split across ESPN, Fox, CBS and NBC. [DATA] [5][6] ESPN Goal Line, the closest past product, shut down in 2020. [DATA] [26] TappedIn works around the rights problem by using channels the viewer already pays for. That is the core positioning.
3. **Every major multiview launch was framed as "choice and control," and the top complaint each time was presets.** YouTube TV (2023), ESPN DTC (2025) and DIRECTV (2026) all launched with fixed game combinations. Fans complained that they couldn't pick their own games. [CONSENSUS] [7][10][14][15] In Oct 2026, ESPN Unlimited's multiview still can't be customized. [DATA] [14]
4. **No incumbent sells automatic switching.** Every multiview product reviewed leaves the viewer to move audio or full-screen by hand. Nothing found in search does automatic break-to-best-game switching, apart from patents. [CONSENSUS] [18][25][33] This is TappedIn's main differentiator. Lead with the switch, not the grid.
5. **When multiview is offered, a large share of viewers use it.** NBCU said more than a quarter of Peacock's Paris Olympics viewers used multiview, splitting time about 50/50 between the featured event and the quad view. [DATA, company-reported] [16] YouTube's CBO called Sunday Ticket multiview its "biggest hit," but gave no numbers. [ANECDOTE]
6. **Big brands launch multiview with press releases, tentpole timing and celebrities, never with a feature-specific campaign.** ESPN DTC launched with John Cena, a mascot and a Publicis media plan, and multiview was a bullet point. [DATA] [12][13] Apple and YouTube debuted multiview around MLB Friday Night Baseball and March Madness. [DATA] [7][18] Tentpole timing is the only part a 2-person team can copy: Rivalry Week, conference championships, March Madness.
7. **A persona plus a catchphrase built RedZone's culture.** Fans picked up "Witching Hour" from Hanson, spread it as a GIF, and wore Hanson Halloween costumes. [ANECDOTE] [2] The transferable part is one repeatable phrase tied to a moment ("the switch"), not a celebrity.
8. **On sports TikTok, highlights with on-screen text are the top format.** TikTok held 39 of the top 50 NFL league posts in 2025. Highlights with on-screen text and reaction prompts led. [DATA] [19] Big caveat: leagues own their footage, and TappedIn does not. Clips of broadcast video are a copyright risk. TappedIn's content has to rely on the app's UI, data tiles, screen recordings of non-broadcast parts, and people reacting.
9. **"Would this get sent to the group chat?" is how Yahoo Fantasy filters posts.** In 2025 Yahoo ran memes and UGC tied to cultural moments, and launched a 6-person creator network. It reports that most viral posts were engaged by non-followers. [ANECDOTE, self-reported awards entry] [21] This is the most transferable content rule in the category.
10. **Sleeper, FotMob and SponsorBlock all grew mostly by word of mouth.** Sleeper: 700–900% year-over-year growth after its 2017 beta, by engaging users on Twitter and Reddit (company claim, 2019). [DATA] [23] FotMob: "almost exclusively word of mouth" per its product lead, with 21M monthly users claimed in its 2026 media kit. [DATA, self-reported] [24] SponsorBlock: a solo student project went from 4 videos in its database to 400+ submissions in 19 days, now about 14k GitHub stars. [DATA] [28]
11. **Indie desktop and open-source apps usually break out on one post in one community.** Wealthfolio (solo dev, Sept 2024) launched quietly on Product Hunt, then hit the Hacker News front page and GitHub Trending, and is now at about 9.2k stars. [DATA] [29] A Reddit launch thread in a niche subreddit brought a small team 750+ iOS installs in a week. [DATA] [30] The pattern: post in the niche where the pain lives, not a general launch site.
12. **Campus apps spread through campus ambassadors.** Fizz launched one school at a time with paid student ambassadors and moderators, plus paid fraternity promotion at one school. [DATA] [31] This fits TappedIn's Xfinity On Campus audience, but Fizz was venture-funded. The unpaid version (friends at each school, RA and club channels) is what transfers.
13. **Awareness of campus TV is low, which helps TappedIn.** Western Washington University dropped Xfinity On Campus after a survey (896 responses) found only 14% of residents used it. [DATA, single school, date unconfirmed] [27] Part of TappedIn's job is reminding students that they already have free live TV.
14. **"Brought my own subscription" overlay tools can scale.** Netflix Party (now Teleparty) adds a layer on top of streaming services the user already pays for. College students and long-distance couples adopted it first, and it claims 10M+ installs. [DATA, company claim via press] [32] This is the closest precedent for TappedIn's "your service, smarter" model.

## 2. Per product

### NFL RedZone (NFL, distributed by ESPN to pay-TV since the 2026 season)
- **Positioning:** Every touchdown from every game, "seven hours of commercial-free football" (2009–2025). From 2025 the opener drops "commercial-free," and Hanson's promise is "we will not miss a touchdown." [1][3] ESPN now distributes it to pay-TV and owns the RedZone brand for other sports. [4]
- **Channels and content:** It relies mostly on the host's persona, catchphrases ("Witching Hour"), fans turning those into GIFs, and fantasy football and betting culture. Hanson credits synced kickoffs and fans whose own team isn't playing. [2]
- **What works:** One consistent ritual (Sunday 1 p.m. ET), a named climax (Witching Hour), and a promise viewers can feel.
- **Transferable:** Name TappedIn's moments ("the switch," "back to your game"). Build habit around Saturday CFB and Sunday NFL windows. **Avoid** using "RedZone" in marketing. ESPN and the NFL own the brand, and implying a connection breaks TappedIn's non-affiliation stance. Phrasing like "red-zone-style" in plain description is the most that is safe.

### YouTube TV multiview / NFL Sunday Ticket
- **Messaging:** "Best sports viewing experience on TV" (2023). Later: "more control over their Sunday viewing" and "build their own multiview" (2025). [7][8][9]
- **Demos:** Blog posts with a GIF, an engineering interview and a short video. The feature launched as "early access" around March Madness. [7]
- **Weakness fans voice:** Presets, games stuck behind other broadcasts, stream limits. [10][11]
- **Transferable:** A short GIF of the UI is the standard demo format. "Early access" framing works for a beta.

### ESPN app / ESPN DTC (Aug 21, 2025)
- **Messaging:** "All of ESPN. All in One Place." Multiview is one bullet among stats, betting and fantasy. [12] Big multi-platform campaign: John Cena, the "App-E" mascot, Complex, Lyft, Samsung, then "Always in Season" in Dec 2025. [13]
- **Reality:** As of Oct 2026, a reviewer says ESPN multiview is "all predetermined," and a Trustpilot reviewer found no college football presets in Week 1. [14] [ANECDOTE]
- **Transferable:** Not the campaign. The gap is what transfers: "pick your own games, any channel your service has" is a concrete comparison point. Keep comparisons factual and dated.

### DIRECTV / Fubo / Apple TV / Peacock
- **DIRECTV:** Oct 2026 custom multiview of up to 4 sports channels, Gemini and Roku only at first. Pitched as "more choice and control." [15]
- **Fubo:** Multiview pitched as pick-any-channel, on select devices (undated guide, weak evidence).
- **Apple:** Up to 4 games on Apple TV 4K, launched as a tvOS 16.5 beta on MLB Friday Night Baseball in 2023. [18]
- **Peacock:** Olympics multiview with more than 25% usage (company-reported), NBA "Courtside Live" camera multiview, and a 2026 vertical mobile multiview. Sold to advertisers as a sponsorable feature. [16][17]
- **Common thread:** Every product is limited to one service's channels and one device class, and all are manual. TappedIn runs on Windows and Mac desktops and laptops, which is where students watch, and switches automatically.

### Fan and fantasy apps (Sleeper, Yahoo Fantasy, ESPN, FotMob, OneFootball, theScore)
- **Sleeper:** Early on, word of mouth plus direct engagement on Twitter and Reddit (2019). Later, a paid creator program ("mid-seven figures," 2023). Job posts call for edgy, trend-driven TikTok and per-team curator accounts. [23] [ANECDOTE for the job posts]
- **Yahoo Fantasy:** Group-chat test, memes, cultural tie-ins, 6 creators. [21]
- **ESPN Creator Network:** Unpaid creators who get access, tickets and creative control. ESPN claims up to 13x benchmark engagement (company claim). [22]
- **FotMob:** Word of mouth first. A 2010 New York Times mention reportedly brought a spike (secondary source). Recently, creator series with Copa90. [24]
- **OneFootball:** Paid TikTok influencer installs, in a paywalled case study. theScore: 1.7M TikTok followers by 2020 and nothing current found (old data).
- **Transferable:** Unpaid creator access works for ESPN because it offers tickets. TappedIn can offer early builds, feature requests, and credit in release notes. Micro-creators who already do "watching 4 games at once" setup content are the natural fit.

## 4. Demand language (paraphrased)

- A fan on X was annoyed that YouTube announced multi-game viewing but picked the games for them. Others wanted to build their own layouts. [10] (2023)
- Sunday Ticket fans were stuck waiting for an overtime game to end. One said he missed 11 minutes of a game he paid for. Another called the feed behavior "so, so, so dumb." [11] (Sept 2026)
- An ESPN app user asked why multiview customization went away and found no pre-built college football options in Week 1. [14] (2025–26)
- A Channels DVR user wanted a recent-channels list to bounce among about 4 channels on big sports days without going back to the guide. [33] (2022)
- A petition to bring back ESPN Goal Line argues it's hard to keep track of many games happening at once without a whip-around show. [26] (undated, few signatures)
- A conference commissioner said a red-zone-style show "incites more engagement," citing shorter attention spans. [6] (Nov 2025)
- Patent filings describe the exact behavior: flip to a channel without a commercial, then return when the original comes back. That suggests long-standing demand but no shipped consumer product. [ANECDOTE]

**Hook ideas from this language:** "Pick your own games." "Never wait out a commercial again." "Your service. Every game. No presets." "The college football RedZone that doesn't exist, built from the TV you already have." (Keep "RedZone" out of ads, see section 2.)

## 5. Weak-evidence claims rejected

- **"816x engagement lift" for on-screen-text highlights** (Net Influencer's summary of the STN report): the multipliers are internally inconsistent (816x vs 9.5x), so only the platform split (39 of the top 50 on TikTok) is used.
- **Rewind's "300k waitlist"**: secondary newsletter only, and Rewind was a16z-funded, so not comparable.
- **Raycast early growth**: no primary account found, and it was YC/Accel-funded. Excluded.
- **Sleeper "near-zero ad spend"**: aggregator only (yespress.io). Only the CNBC-attributed claim is kept.
- **FotMob's NYT spike, "hundreds of thousands of downloads overnight"**: secondary blog. Mentioned as unverified.
- **MultiTwitch / Multistre.am origin stories**: nothing found. Omitted.
- **Any 2025–26 RedZone viewership or subscriber figures**: none published.
- **Sunday Ticket multiview usage share**: no figure published.
