## 1. Method

- Tool: playwright-core 1.64.1 (already installed in the repo), system Chrome, headless, fresh non-persistent context, viewport 1440x900, locale en-US. No logins, no accounts, nothing installed. No cookie banner appeared on Meta, TikTok Library or Google ATC, so none was accepted.
- **Meta Ad Library** (country US, ad type All ads, active only). Advertisers were picked from the search box's "Advertisers" typeahead, which opens the page view (`search_type=page&view_all_page_id=…`). Keywords used exact-phrase search (`search_type=keyword_exact_phrase`). The script scrolled until no new cards loaded (6 to 8 scrolls), then parsed every loaded card: start date, format (a `<video>` element in the card means video, otherwise image or carousel), primary text, link caption, CTA and video duration (from the player's "0:00 / m:ss" label). It took element screenshots of the oldest cards.
  - Limits: Meta's Sort menu only offers "Impressions: high to low" and "Most recent". There is **no oldest-first sort**, so "longest-running" means the oldest card among those loaded. For NFL (122 of ~340 loaded), ESPN (67 of ~90), Sling (45 of ~60), DraftKings Sports (118 of ~170) and theScore Bet (28 of ~61), an older ad may exist that never loaded. "~N results" counts ads, and one card can group several ads ("N ads use this creative and text"), so loaded cards are fewer than results.
  - "60+ days" means the ad started on or before Aug 22, 2026.
  - Counts below are cards, and variants of one creative are counted separately. I give distinct-creative counts where they matter.
- **TikTok Creative Center**: Top Ads (`…/inspiration/topads/pc/en`, with and without `?period=30&region=US`) and Trending Hashtags (`…/popular/hashtag/pc/en`) both returned **HTTP 403, "Access to ads.tiktok.com was denied"**. I did not try to get around it (no other browser, no stealth, no login). Trending songs and creators are on the same domain and were not tried. **Skipped: blocked (403).** Screenshot: `tiktok-cc-topads-blocked-403.jpg`.
- **TikTok Commercial Content Library** (library.tiktok.com/ads): loaded fine. Region "All countries"; last-shown window 9/21/2026 to 10/21/2026 (the default).
- **Google Ads Transparency Center**: region US, advertiser picked from the typeahead.
- **Not done**: Snap and Reddit libraries (not checked, so I have no finding either way). The Meta pages for "Sleeper" (fantasy app) and "theScore" (media app, not Bet) did not show up in the typeahead.

## 7. Patterns across everything

n = number of loaded Meta cards unless noted (variants counted). Patterns based on fewer than 5 distinct creatives or advertisers are marked **thin evidence**.

**All active ads**
1. **The CTA follows the business model.** Subscription TV uses "Sign Up" (ESPN 52 of 67, Sling 39 of 45). App installs use "Install Now"/"Download" (DraftKings 105 of 118, FanDuel 18 of 18, theScore Bet, CFL app). Fubo and NFL use "Learn more" (n = 5 advertisers, 300+ cards). Screens: `meta-espn-ad-Sep22026.jpg`, `meta-fanduel-ad-Sep42026.jpg`, `meta-thescorebet-ad-Sep172026-1.jpg`.
2. **Short, sport- or season-specific one-liners with a leading emoji** ("🏈 Game day starts here.", "🏀 NBA season is live on Fubo.", "🏒 New NHL Season…") from Fubo (29 cards), FanDuel (7 of 18) and theScore Bet. n = 3 advertisers, so thin evidence across advertisers but consistent within them. `meta-fubo-ad-gamedaystartshere.jpg`.
3. **"Never miss / don't miss" fear-of-missing-out lines**: ESPN ("Never miss a moment" in 20 of 67 cards; "miss" appears in 35 of 67, "Don't Miss Out on ESPN", "you don't have to miss them"), Sling ("Don't miss your football games"), theScore Bet ("Don't miss the action"). n = 3 advertisers. `meta-espn-results.jpg`.
4. **Quantity claims**: "1,050+ out-of-market games", "8+ Leagues. 1 ESPN App.", "200+ Channels", "10+ Live Channels", "380 Matches". ESPN, Fubo (Google), Sling (Google). n = 3 advertisers.
5. **Price anchoring**: Sling "starting at $19.99/mo." (most Sling cards), Fubo "SAVE $30" / "$30 off your first month", bonus offers in betting ("Spend $5 Get $200", "$1,000 First Bet Reset"). n = 4 advertisers.
6. **Creator and partner co-posts ("X with Brand")**: ESPN (Simone's Playbook, plus sponsor brands), Sling (MrG030, OGlightskins, The College Sports Company, Richard Sherman Podcast), DraftKings (Kevin Hart, Barstool, Chad Johnson), NFL (Colleen Wolfe). Creator videos run longer (43 to 98 s) than brand cuts (6 to 15 s). n = 4 advertisers. `meta-slingtv-ad-creator-mrg030.jpg`, `meta-espn-ad-Sep122026.jpg`.
7. **Lengths**: brand videos cluster at 15 s (ESPN 11 of 32, Sling 7 of 23, DraftKings 25 of 53) and 6 to 10 s (NFL 95 of 108 at 6 s; Sling 10 s ×5). n = 4 advertisers.
8. **Many near-identical variants** (state names, team names, languages): DraftKings "Now Live in [State]", NFL Extra Points per team, Fubo English/Spanish. n = 3 advertisers.
9. **Formats**: comparables split about evenly between image and video (ESPN 35/32, Sling 22/23, DraftKings 65/53, FanDuel 10/8). Fubo is almost all image (28/29) and NFL almost all video (108/122). Carousels are rare (none among comparables; only in keyword results).

11. **The multiview visual (2x2 grid)** appeared only in hardware ads (AWOL Vision). **Thin evidence (n=1).**

## 8. What transfers to TappedIn (free app, tiny team, no footage or league/network logos)

**Patterns that depend on footage or logos (don't copy):** Fubo's network-logo grid (`meta-fubo-ad-gamedaystartshere.jpg`, `google-atc-fubo.jpg`), Sling's RedZone creative with player photos and the RedZone logo (`meta-slingtv-ad-nflredzone.jpg`), ESPN banners with team, league and player imagery (`google-atc-espn.jpg`), FanDuel's player art, the DIRECTV dealer's game-action photo, AWOL's 2x2 grid of real football broadcasts. The unbranded "TV service" reseller ads (`meta-kw-multiview-ad-iptv-stopscrolling.jpg`) are a style to stay well away from: huge channel counts, a cartoon superhero, "Send message".

**Patterns that don't need footage or logos (usable):**
1. **The problem-first frame no one in the app space owns**: "Game day should be about watching football, not searching for it" (DIRECTV dealer) and "Where's My Game?" (Spectrum) show distributors already selling *less searching*. TappedIn's angle (it switches for you during commercials) wasn't seen in any ad. Thin evidence (n=2), but cheap to test with copy alone.
2. **Plain one-line, emoji-led, season-specific hooks** (Fubo, FanDuel, theScore Bet): easy for a tiny team to rotate by sport ("🏈 Saturday, four games, zero commercials", for example). n=3 advertisers.
3. **Short video lengths**: 6 to 15 s brand cuts dominate (n=4 advertisers). TappedIn can make 6 to 15 s screen recordings with mock or data tiles (its own `--dry-run` / replay data-tile mode), so no broadcast video is needed.
4. **Generic grids instead of logos**: a 2x2 layout with blurred or abstract tiles and score-bug-style text gets the "multiview" idea across without footage (inference from AWOL's visual; thin evidence, n=1).
5. **CTA**: install-style apps use "Download"/"Install Now"; long-running evergreen ads used "Learn more". For a free desktop app, "Download" fits the pattern of app advertisers (n=4: DraftKings, FanDuel, theScore Bet, CFL).
6. **Creator co-posts** are used by every subscription comparable (n=4) and run 40 to 100 s in a native, talking-to-camera style. That is a cheap fit for student creators, as long as they film their own reaction and screen, not the broadcast.
7. **Evergreen plus rotating**: the only comparable ads running 60+ days were evergreen statements (thin evidence, n=3). A reasonable setup is one evergreen "never hunt for the good game again" ad plus weekly sport-specific variants.
8. **Campus angle**: Pavlov Media (a student-housing ISP) is already advertising a free "Game Day Bundle — NFL+ & Peacock" (`meta-kw-nflplus-ad-Sep22026.jpg`), and AccessParks targets ads by community name. Campus and housing ISPs are both an audience and a possible partner channel (n=2, thin evidence).
9. **Channels**: Meta's library is the only usable US source for competitor creative. TikTok's US Creative Center data was blocked (403), and TikTok's library has no US coverage. Google ATC shows Fubo and Sling relying on search text ads ("Start Your Free Trial", "No Long-Term Contracts"). A free app can borrow "Free. No sign-up." style search copy (n=2).
