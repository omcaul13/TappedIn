Release notes for

    Your own red zone channel

    TappedIn follows ESPN's live game data and changes channels on your TV service the way you would, only faster and without missing a snap.

    Up to four games, one window

    Each screen follows its own game, and each can be a different sport: college football on two screens, the NHL on the third, the NBA on the fourth. Pick from today's games, search by team or network, and see at a glance which ones your lineup carries.

    Commercial break? Switch.

    When a game goes to a long break (end of a quarter, a TV timeout, a kickoff after a score), its screen fills in with the most exciting live game. It goes back as soon as your game shows a new play. Ordinary stoppages like incompletions and penalties never trigger a switch.

    The best game, in three tiers

    When a screen needs a fill-in, TappedIn looks for one in this order, and every switch tells you which tier it came from.

    1. Same sportRed zone, power play, clutch time, match point: that sport's biggest moments first.
    2. A sport another screen is watchingRanked by a 0–100 excitement score: how close, how late, and what's happening.
    3. Any sport you followA tight soccer match is a great way to wait out a football timeout.

    Halftime highlights

    At halftime or a hockey intermission, the screen plays a highlights reel (same sport first) and a scores board while the game keeps going underneath, so the second half starts instantly.

    Game over? Pick the next one

    When a game ends, a picker appears in that screen with what's live now, same sport first, and a countdown. Pick one, or let TappedIn put the best live game on for you.

    Fullscreen any screen

    Double-click a screen (or press 1–4) to fill the window. The others keep playing and switching behind it, so going back is instant.

    Big and mini tickers

    A scrolling score ticker across the top for every sport, or a one-line mini ticker by the clock that jumps to scores and big moments.

    You stay in control

    Lock a screen so it never switches, hit "Back to my game" any time, and choose whose sound you hear. Switching sensitivity goes from Low (only clear long breaks, the default) to High (most stoppages, and jumps to big moments), for every sport or one at a time.

    Supported sports

    Each sport has its own break detector, built from that sport's measured stoppages, so TappedIn knows a basketball TV timeout from a foul shot.

    How it works

    Setup takes about two minutes the first time.

    1. Pick your service

      Choose your TV service, or search for your school to find its campus TV.

    2. Sign in on its own page

      TappedIn opens your service's real sign-in page. Your school's SSO and Duo work as usual.

    3. Choose your games

      TappedIn checks your channels and how many streams your plan plays, then picks great games to start. Change any screen whenever you like.

    4. Watch

      Sit back. Breaks, halftimes and finals take care of themselves.

    Questions

    Do I need a subscription?

    Yes. TappedIn doesn't provide any channels. It plays the channels you already get from your own campus TV service (for example Xfinity On Campus through your housing) or a TV service you subscribe to. Games on networks your lineup doesn't carry are greyed out and never picked.

    Is my password safe?

    TappedIn never sees your password. You sign in on your service's own page, in a window TappedIn opens, exactly as you would in a browser. TappedIn never types, stores or sends your password, and it never accepts terms or agreements for you. Only the browser's normal sign-in cookies are kept, on your computer, separately for each service.

    Why didn't it switch during a commercial?

    ESPN's live data doesn't say when a commercial starts, so TappedIn forecasts how long each stoppage will last from real stoppages measured in recorded games. It's a very good guess, not a certainty: on Low (the default) it catches about 80% of long college-football breaks while skipping short ones, and ESPN's data runs about 25 seconds behind. Choose Medium or High in Settings to switch for more breaks, or use a screen's game list to switch yourself.

    My service isn't listed, or it doesn't work. What can I do?

    Experimental services sometimes need a fix when their website changes. Open Diagnostics (from setup, or Settings → TV service): it tests each step and lets you correct what fails, no coding needed. Then send us the export so everyone gets the fix. See fix your service. If your service isn't listed at all, email us and tell us which one.

    Is there a Mac version?

    Not yet. TappedIn runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

    Does it record or share the games?

    No. TappedIn plays your service's own player and changes channels the way you would. It never records, extracts or redistributes video and never touches copy protection. Everything runs on your computer.

    How many streams does it use?

    One per screen. During setup TappedIn adds screens one at a time to see how many your plan plays at once, then sizes the grid to fit (1 to 4 screens) and tells you why.
